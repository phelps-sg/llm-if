Planetfall by Steve Meretzky
As played on ifMUD on April 3, 2022 April 3, 2022

The idea behind ClubFloyd is that each week at a pre-arranged time, a group of people meet online to cooperatively play a game of interactive fiction.

Below is a transcript of Planetfall written by Steve Meretzky. Planetfall was released in 1983 by Infocom. Planetfall was released in 1983 by Infocom. You can learn more about the game, including how to download it, by visiting IFDB.

WARNING! Below you will find a transcript of people playing this game, and it goes without saying that the transcript is full of spoilers. So, if you've never played this game, and think you might like to at some point, I do not recommend reading any further. Instead, you might want to return to the interactive fiction page.
